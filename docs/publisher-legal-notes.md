Updated 23 September 2026. This is an implementation record, not a certification of legal compliance.

- Public canonical domain: **https://mkrting.com**. Vercel redirects `www.mkrting.com` to it. Sitemap, robots file, page canonicals, feeds and structured data must use the apex.

## What the public pages cover

- `/contact/`: email, LinkedIn, correction and permission routes.
- `/privacy/`: hosting request data, correspondence, optional Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights, Google Fonts, external links, service providers and request rights.
- `/terms/`: editorial nature of the content, accuracy, copyright, brand ownership, external sites, availability and contact.
- `/method/` and `/corrections/`: evidence standards, AI assistance, human editorial review and factual corrections.

The site currently has no reader accounts, comment form, newsletter or advertising tracker in its source. Update the policy before adding any of those features. Vercel describes its Web Analytics as aggregate and cookie free; do not extend that statement to every possible future tool. See [Vercel privacy documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy).

## Items requiring owner or counsel review

1. If the publication is operated under a registered legal entity, replace the public operator name with its exact registered name and add any legally required contact details.
2. Review whether the publication's coverage qualifies as digital news and current affairs under India's IT Rules. If it does, obtain advice on publisher and grievance requirements before claiming compliance. The [Ministry of Information and Broadcasting's Digital Media page](https://www.mib.gov.in/en/node/1356?page=1) describes the rules and grievance system.
3. Review the privacy notice against the actual services enabled in Vercel and Gmail whenever those settings change. India's [Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf) and [commencement notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf) have staged effective dates; do not label this site fully compliant without a legal and operational review.
4. Review permissions for any campaign images, logos, embeds or third-party text added later. Crediting a source does not itself grant reuse permission.

Google's [Organization markup guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization) supports a real external `sameAs` link and a sufficiently large crawlable logo. The site points those fields at the owner-provided LinkedIn page and a 512 pixel version of the existing mark; no unprovided `legalName` or address is invented.
